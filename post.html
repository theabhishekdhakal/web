Choosing a CMS in 2026

By Name Surname28 Sep 20264 min read

Start with your people, not the tool. Ask who edits the site every week and what slows them down.

Look at the daily work

A good system makes the common task obvious and the rare task possible.

The best CMS is the one your team forgets they are using.

A short checklist

  • Can a new hire publish in a day?
  • Can you export everything?
  • Is it fast on a phone?
